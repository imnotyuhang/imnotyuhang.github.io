## What It Does

The system integrates hybrid robotic arms, an end effector, and pose estimation to support motion sensing, therapy trajectory tracking, and replay of therapeutic movement.

## My Work

I worked on the motion sensing and analysis side, improving motion prediction through signal filtering and model-based correction while analyzing sequential tracking data for compliance and engagement.
