## Modeling

The project used an LSTM-ARIMA hybrid model for extreme heat prediction across time horizons, with incremental learning to improve short-term forecasting.

## Health-risk Analysis

DLNM quantified pollution-health lag effects, while GAM helped assess heat-pollution health synergies across cities.
