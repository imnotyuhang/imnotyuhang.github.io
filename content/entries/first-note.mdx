## A starting point

A personal site should not only answer whether someone is employable. It should also keep traces of what they are learning, building, noticing, and becoming.

This site starts with a resume and several AI/data projects, but the longer-term direction is broader: a technical notebook, a project archive, and a place to keep reflections that would not fit into a CV.

The practical goal is still simple: make the important things easy to find. The personal goal is to let the site feel alive enough that it is worth returning to.
