Project / Robotics

Robot Therapist for Assisted Therapeutic Exercises

A robot-assisted rehabilitation system for upper-limb stroke therapy, built around motion sensing, trajectory tracking, therapeutic replay, and engagement analysis.

MediaPipe BlazePose Robotics Rehabilitation

What It Does

The system integrates hybrid robotic arms, an end effector, and pose estimation to support motion sensing, therapy trajectory tracking, and replay of therapeutic movement.

My Work

I worked on the motion sensing and analysis side, improving motion prediction through signal filtering and model-based correction while analyzing sequential tracking data for compliance and engagement.

Next Details To Add

Robot photos, sensing pipeline, trajectory examples, and therapy workflow diagrams can turn this into a fuller case study.
